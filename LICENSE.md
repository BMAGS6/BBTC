## 1. PURPOSE AND PRINCIPLES

BBTC was created principally as a work of scientific, educational, technical,
and software-engineering experimentation.

The purpose of this License is to make the Software broadly available for
lawful personal, academic, educational, recreational, experimental, research,
and other noncommercial civilian purposes while reserving commercial
exploitation and professional institutional uses connected with military
operations, law enforcement, mercenary activity, and similar armed
professional activities to separate authorization by the Licensor.

The philosophy underlying this License is informed in part by traditions of
American civic self-reliance, civilian marksmanship, individual
responsibility, community preparedness, and civil defense, together with the
historical American distinction between professional armed institutions and
the broader civilian population capable of assuming civic responsibilities in
extraordinary circumstances.

Among the historical and legal concepts informing this philosophy is that of
the American militia, including the distinction between organized military
forces and the broader body of civilians historically understood as capable of
lawful collective defense. The contemporary American legal concept of the
unorganized militia is one example of this distinction.

These historical influences do not limit the permissions of this License to
the United States or to United States citizens. Subject to applicable law,
lawful civilian users and qualifying civilian organizations of every
nationality are intended to receive the same permissions under the same
conditions.

This License is intended to preserve broad lawful civilian access to the
