# BBTC Safety and Model Disclaimer

BBTC is experimental software for education, research, simulation, and game
development. Its outputs are mathematical predictions produced by simplified
models and numerical methods. They are not physical measurements.

BBTC does not publish loading data, certify ammunition, approve component
combinations, or determine that any cartridge, propellant charge, firearm, or
procedure is safe. No reported pressure, velocity, energy, warning, validity
flag, or absence of a warning should be interpreted as a declaration of safety.

Real internal-ballistics behavior depends on component lots, case volume,
primer behavior, propellant condition, projectile construction, seating depth,
chamber and bore geometry, temperature, manufacturing tolerances, measurement
method, and other effects that a model may omit or approximate. Small input
changes can produce large pressure changes.

Do not use BBTC output as a substitute for:

- published load data from reputable component or ammunition manufacturers;
- the instructions and limits in current reloading manuals;
- qualified professional judgment;
- standardized pressure-testing equipment; or
- applicable laws, regulations, and safety practices.

Never infer a new real-world propellant charge or exceed published limits
because a simulation appears favorable. Any future Cubes of Honor gameplay
logic derived from BBTC is fictional game behavior and is not real-world
guidance.

Model assumptions, applicability limits, warnings, and validation evidence must
be reviewed alongside every result. Passing software tests does not establish
physical validation.
